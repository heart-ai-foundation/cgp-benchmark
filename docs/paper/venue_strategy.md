# Venue Strategy

## Recommendation

Use a preprint-first strategy, then fork the manuscript into a software-engineering venue version if the paper is submitted for peer review.

The immediate goal is not only academic placement. The practical goal is a public, citable, integrity-preserving artifact that supports a reliability and auditability consulting offer. A preprint-first workflow serves that goal better than delaying the paper for a specific venue template.

## Primary Track

The primary manuscript should remain an empirical software-engineering benchmark paper. Its strongest contribution is not a broad AI ethics claim and not a confirmed drift-reduction result. Its strongest contribution is an open, reproducible benchmark showing how a continuity-governance scaffold changes operational reliability, evidence production, and failure-mode visibility across coding-agent runs while honestly reporting a null registered endpoint.

Best-fit future venue family:

- Software engineering in practice tracks.
- Empirical software engineering venues.
- Software-agent evaluation workshops attached to major software engineering conferences.

This version should emphasize:

- controlled benchmark design;
- preregistration and deviation handling;
- isolated worktree execution;
- run-capture and evidence artifacts;
- registered endpoint null at floor;
- operational validity and non-submission detection;
- implications for organizations adopting coding agents.

## Secondary Fork

A later governance-oriented fork may be suitable if the emphasis shifts from coding-agent benchmarking to evidence governance and claims integrity.

Possible venue family:

- accountability, transparency, or responsible-AI venues;
- AI governance workshops;
- human-centered AI operations venues.

That version should emphasize:

- auditability;
- claims boundaries;
- public deviation notes;
- dual-entity evidence integrity;
- how benchmark evidence should and should not be used in commercial claims.

This fork should not be the first target unless the manuscript is substantially reframed.

## Current Paper Positioning

Recommended title:

Reliability and Auditability Effects of Continuity-Governed Prompting: A Controlled Benchmark of Agent-Assisted Coding Workflows

Recommended contribution statement:

This paper presents a preregistered, open, controlled benchmark of continuity governance in agent-assisted coding workflows. The registered drift endpoint returned a null result at a baseline floor. The benchmark nevertheless produced operational evidence that a governance scaffold can improve valid completion, work submission, evidence production, and failure-mode visibility in a baseline-unreliable coding-agent platform.

## Do Not Lead With

- "CGP reduced drift."
- "CGP passed the benchmark."
- "Benchmark-proven drift reduction."
- A venue frame that forces the paper to overstate the governance or ethics contribution before the empirical software-engineering contribution is complete.

Prepare a preprint-ready Markdown/PDF package, attach the v1.1 deviation note to OSF, and use the public repository plus OSF record as the stable citation base. After that, fork only the cover letter and introduction/discussion emphasis for a specific venue.
